📜 I. GENERAL CONDUCT

  • Respect Everyone: Zero tolerance for harassment, toxicity, bullying, or hate speech.

💬 II. CHAT & PLATFORM SAFETY

  • Platform TOS: Follow both Discord Terms of Service & Roblox Terms of Service.

  • Staff Authority: Follow staff directions. If you have a dispute, handle it privately via tickets/DMs. Staff have final say in all disciplinary actions (warnings, mutes, kicks, bans).
